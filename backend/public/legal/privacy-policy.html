Remind Me This Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Remind Me This ("we", "our", or "us") provides reminders, calendar sync, and notification services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, name, phone number, address, and birth date if you provide it.
  • Event and reminder information that you create in the app.
  • Calendar sync and integration metadata that is needed to connect external providers.
  • Support messages that you send through Contact Us.
  • Technical logs needed for reliability, fraud prevention, and security.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To deliver reminders and notifications you request.
  • To sync with connected calendar providers.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve app reliability and security.

SMS and Text Messaging (Twilio)

If you enable SMS reminders, we send text messages using Twilio as our messaging service provider. Your mobile number and reminder message content are processed to deliver requested reminders.

SMS consent and preferences

  • You only receive SMS reminders if you choose to enable SMS delivery.
  • Message frequency varies based on your reminders and settings.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out of SMS reminders at any time by replying STOP to a message.
  • You can request help by replying HELP to a message.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate the app (for example, cloud hosting, email delivery, and SMS delivery) and only for business purposes.

Data Retention

We retain account, reminder, and related service data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

  • Update account details in the app.
  • Enable or disable SMS, email, or device reminders in app settings.
  • Delete your account from within the app.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Remind Me This is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and update the effective date.
