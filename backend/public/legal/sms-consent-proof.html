Remind Me This SMS CTA and Opt-In Proof

This page is publicly hosted for campaign verification and demonstrates the exact SMS opt-in flow shown to users during app registration.

Where users sign up

Users create an account inside the iOS app named Remind Me This.

The sign-up flow is not behind a website login page. The CTA is shown in-app at account creation. This page and the screenshots below are provided as reviewer-accessible proof.

Exact SMS CTA shown to users

SMS reminders consent *

Reply choice in app: Y for Yes or N for No. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

Consent is explicit and optional. Users must actively choose Y or N. No pre-checked consent is used.
